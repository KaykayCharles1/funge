# Known issues

- **The game server runs the clock.** If it goes down, grabs stop. Nobody can steal the pot: after 12 blocks (about two hours) anyone can settle and the last holder is paid. A page that cannot reach the server shows that wait and a Settle button when it is ready. A round nobody has joined only ends by the referee cancelling it or after 144 blocks (about a day).
- **Grabs from your own wallet can be late.** The grab is judged when it reaches the server, and with a WizardConnect wallet your approval time counts. A last-second approval is refused as too late. The game wallet prepares coins ahead of time, so a click a couple of seconds before the end still works.
- **Outage Settle needs the game wallet.** When the server is down, the Settle button uses the game wallet to pay the network fee, so a player with only an own wallet cannot use it.
- **External wallet pairing (WizardConnect).** After pasting the link into a wallet, the page may keep showing the QR code until you reload it, and after a reload "Reconnecting to your wallet" may only finish once you switch to the wallet tab and back. We suspect the pairing library drops its relay connection while the tab is in the background and ignores older messages after reconnecting; this is unconfirmed. The game wallet does not use pairing.
- **One PUSD coin per grab.** A grab spends a single PUSD coin that covers the price. If a wallet's PUSD is split across several small coins, the page asks the player to combine them first.
- **Game wallet limits.** There is no Withdraw button yet, and trophy NFTs cannot be sent out of the game wallet. Funds and trophies can be reached by restoring the seed phrase in another wallet.
- **The fallback wait is an estimate.** The server's clock is exact, but the no-server fallback counts blocks, and blocks arrive at random (about 10 minutes on average), so its "about N minutes" can be off by a lot.
- **Public chipnet Electrum servers are few and slow.** Only one or two answer on the default secure WebSocket port, and their response time varies from a couple of seconds to ten. The app tries several servers and remembers the one that answered, but a total outage shows a "network servers are not responding" message. Browsers also print their own "WebSocket connection failed" lines for servers that do not answer; the page cannot hide those.
- **The server's clock must be right.** Deadlines come from the host's clock, so the host must keep its time synced.
