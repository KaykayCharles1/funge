# Landing page

Scope: the Funge landing page (`landing.html`), separate from the game. Visitor mode: Persuade. Built section by section: the hero first, approved by the owner before anything below it changes.

Audience and job: hackathon judges and players, equally. Understand the rules in seconds, trust who pays, and take one action: Join live battle (`/?mode=battlefield`).

Sections (confirmed): hero; the rules, plainly; why you can trust it; how to start. No live round data, no network mention, no links besides the battle and the page's own sections. Avoid: crypto hype, a kids' game look, corporate SaaS coldness, long text, emojis, framed and bordered boxes everywhere.

Owner's references (binding): Duna, Cap, Anything (one painted or photographic scene at the top that fades into a clean light page), Tinkeroll (a big confident headline over a soft game world), Cap (the real product shown in a frame overlapping the scene's edge). Light theme below the hero, with dark as the fallback if light does not work. The owner's logo (crown over FUNGE) is the brand mark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the painted valley is the sky the page opens in; the real battlefield, shown in a clean frame across the line where the painting meets the page, proves the game exists. It refuses the dark framed box and the crypto landing template.

OWN-WORLD: a light sky-white page (#f5f8fc family) under a saturated painted sky; navy ink for text on light, white on the sky; gold only as accent from the logo's crown; the owner's logo as the one display element; one humanist sans (Alegreya Sans) for everything else; pill-shaped controls, soft offset shadows, no borders around everything.

STORY: the visitor meets the logo and one line in the open sky, sees four armies converging on the castle, then the real battlefield in a frame, and takes the one action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a slim pill bar at the top (logo mark left, the page's section links in the middle, Join live battle right); the logo centred in the sky, the headline under it, one line of explanation, Join live battle as the primary button and How it works as the secondary; the painting full width, its foot fading into the light page; the top of the battlefield frame rising over that fade at the bottom of the viewport. Phones: the sky as a soft band holding the logo, headline and buttons, the whole painting at its native shape under it, the frame below.

FORM: owner-pinned direction from references (replaces the box-lid direction); seed:876758d959d54e45 kept for the record. Signature interaction: the battlefield frame rises a little as the page scrolls past the painting (static with reduced motion).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
